Published Papers

Prediction of index futures movement using TimeGAN and 3D-CNN: Empirical evidence from Korea and the United States

Woojung Kim, Jiyoung Jeon, Sanghoe Kim, Minwoo Jang, Heesoo Lee, Sanghyuk Yoo, and Kyong Joo Oh

Applied Soft Computing, 171, 112748 · 2025

Publisher

This paper develops an algorithmic trading framework for Korean and U.S. index futures by combining TimeGAN-based time-series augmentation with a three-dimensional convolutional neural network. The empirical analysis evaluates its predictive and trading performance across both markets under realistic futures-market conditions.
